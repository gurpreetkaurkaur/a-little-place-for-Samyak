A letter from Preet, just for you.

My Samyak, please read this slowly. 🩷

If you're opening this because you feel unloved, unimportant, or maybe just a little low, I wish I could sit beside you and remind you of everything I sometimes fail to say properly.

You are so, so important to me. Not only when you're smiling, joking, being cute or having a good day. You matter on your quiet days too. You matter when you're tired, confused, irritated, emotional, or when you don't even know how to explain what you're feeling.

You do not have to earn my care by being perfect. You don't have to always be strong, funny, available or okay. You're a human being, and you are allowed to have feelings. I want you to feel safe enough to be honest about them.

Before you think anything negative about me…

Please, before you assume that I don't care, that you're not important to me, or that something has changed, talk to me first. Mere baare mein kuch negative sochne se pehle, please give me a chance to explain. Ask me what's wrong. Tell me what made you feel that way. I would rather have an honest conversation than let a misunderstanding grow between us.

Sometimes I may be busy, distracted, tired, or not very good at expressing what's in my head. Sometimes my words may come out wrong. That doesn't mean your feelings are silly, and it doesn't mean we should ignore what hurt. We can talk about it, listen to each other and understand each other. I don't expect either of us to read minds.

Please don't decide what I feel without letting me speak. And I promise to try to listen to you too, instead of making you feel that you have to explain your heart a hundred times.

If you ever feel you're not enough…

You are not in a competition with anybody. You don't have to compare yourself to anyone or prove that you deserve love. I appreciate the person you are: your silly side, your little habits, your random talks, your dreams, your efforts and all the tiny things that make you Samyak.

I hope you see your own worth, even on days when confidence feels far away. I hope you are kinder to yourself. I'm proud of you for continuing to learn, grow and try, even when things don't go exactly as planned.

When life feels too heavy…

Take one thing at a time, okay? You don't have to solve your whole life in one night. Rest when you need to. Eat something, drink some water, get some fresh air, and give yourself a little patience. You deserve support, not pressure. If things feel bigger than you can manage alone, please reach out to someone you trust. You don't have to carry everything silently.

When you miss me…

Think of my random messages, my silly questions, the way I want to know how your day went, and all the little moments that are ours. I hope we keep making memories, laughing at nonsense and finding new reasons to make each other smile.

What I want you to know most

I can't promise that we'll never have misunderstandings or difficult days. No relationship is perfect. But I do want us to keep choosing honesty, kindness, patience and effort. Your feelings deserve respect, and so do mine. We should be able to talk without fear, listen without dismissing each other, and make space for both of us to grow.

I want good things for you, Samyak. I want you to feel proud of yourself, chase the things that make you happy, reach your goals and have a life filled with peace, laughter and people who value you. Please don't forget that you are more than one bad day, one mistake or one difficult moment.

And if you ever forget all of this, come back and read this again. This little place will be here to remind you: You matter, you are appreciated, and you deserve to feel loved.

Lots and lots of love,
Preet ♡ 🎀
